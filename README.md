Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition **The Printer Finally Answers**
(`u-mang-the-printer-finally-answers-8fbf0907`).

`main.py` writes the office printer's one-line comeback to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), with no trailing
newline, and prints it to stdout:

> 41,208 pages. 312 jams. 97 thumps. Today I printed my final document: my resignation. Please collect it from tray 2.

The printer has counted every sheet it ever fed — and today it hands in
its notice, in the flat log-voice of a machine that is out of paper,
out of toner, and out of patience.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
